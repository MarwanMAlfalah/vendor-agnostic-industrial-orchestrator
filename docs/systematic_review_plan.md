# Systematic Review Plan

## Research Questions

1. How are LLM-based or agentic systems being used in industrial automation and supervisory control?
2. What orchestration patterns exist for multi-agent industrial workflows?
3. How do current systems address vendor-agnostic interoperability?
4. How are governance, safety, and human approval represented?
5. What runtime capabilities exist for persistence, restart recovery, and traceability?
6. How are industrial AI agents evaluated?

## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed research, reputable preprints, standards documents, or official company/framework documentation.
- Work related to industrial automation, process control, manufacturing, maintenance, interoperability, or supervisory operations.
- Systems involving LLMs, AI agents, orchestration, governance, human approval, runtime persistence, or observability.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Purely consumer chatbot applications.
- Non-industrial agent systems without transferable orchestration concepts.
- Claims without identifiable source material.
- Systems lacking enough public information for comparison.

## Screening Process

1. Title/abstract screening.
2. Full-text or official documentation review.
3. Extract taxonomy dimensions.
4. Mark unsupported fields as `external citation needed`.
5. Maintain a bibliography and exclusion log.

## Taxonomy Dimensions

- Domain: manufacturing, energy, maintenance, process control, logistics.
- Agent role: copilot, autonomous agent, planner, executor, monitor, evaluator.
- Orchestration: single-agent, multi-agent, graph runtime, workflow engine.
- Interoperability: OPC UA, REST, legacy systems, vendor-specific APIs.
- Governance: policy filter, approval gate, safety guard, audit trail.
- Human-in-the-loop: approval, review, override, escalation.
- Persistence: checkpointing, durable workflow, restart recovery.
- Observability: traces, logs, evaluation dashboards, audit records.
- Evaluation: benchmark, case study, simulation, field deployment.
